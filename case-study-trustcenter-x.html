The problem

Product thesis: provide it once, then reuse it across engagements.

01 · Context and opportunity

One vendor. Multiple client tenants. The same work repeated.

Certa is a client-facing third-party risk platform. Each client operates in a separate tenant and onboards vendors through its own engagements and evaluation workflows.

When the same vendor participated in another engagement, the workflow started again. Company data was entered again. The same compliance documents were uploaded again. Information already available to Certa could not be reused as a persistent vendor resource.

Where we started

Provide once. Reuse across engagements.

The initial thesis was focused: let vendors maintain their data and documents once, then reuse the same information wherever appropriate instead of repeating intake.

A broader supplier-side product

Reuse solved the immediate workflow problem. Research showed that the vendor side could also receive standalone value—and create a path to monetization.

02 · Product discovery

Research narrowed the opportunity to three pillars

I began by understanding the repeated-submission problem and what value a dedicated supplier experience could create. The research produced one product vision with three connected jobs.

01 · Foundation

Reuse

Maintain vendor documents and data once, then reuse them across eligible engagements.

02 · Share

Trust center

Give vendors a shareable view of their documents and compliance posture during evaluation.

03 · Prepare

Self-audit

Help vendors assess their compliance posture and identify gaps before evaluation.

03 · Full-product prototype

Make the complete vision tangible

I built an end-to-end clickable prototype covering all three pillars. It gave the internal leadership team a complete product experience to evaluate—not a collection of disconnected feature ideas.

Three pillars. One portal.

The concept brought reusable data, a shareable trust center and compliance self-audit into one supplier experience. Leadership approved the direction, moving the work into client-specific pilot discovery.

04 · Client-specific discovery

Find a pilot without changing client operations

After leadership approved the complete concept, I interviewed stakeholders across four client environments. I mapped each onboarding flow, tested how visible the repeated-upload problem was and looked for a pilot where the client-side process could remain intact.

05 · Pilot design

The approved product vision contained three pillars. The pilot deliberately contained one. Data reuse—and the separate supplier portal required to support it—was the foundation that needed to work before adding the trust center or self-audit.
